# UN Digital Cooperation Portal — Operational Human Oversight for Agentic AI

## Overview

This page documents a public contribution by **Lei Gao** listed on the **UN Digital Cooperation Portal** in support of implementation of the Global Digital Compact.

The contribution is titled:

**Operational Human Oversight Integration for Agentic AI Trust Systems**

It focuses on how meaningful human intervention, accountability, traceability, and governance safeguards can be operationally structured for increasingly autonomous and agentic AI systems.

---

## Research Focus

The contribution addresses Operational Human Oversight as a practical governance problem rather than only a high-level ethical principle.

Key areas include:

- human intervention triggers
- escalation to qualified human review
- human authority and override
- temporary hold or suspension
- resumption and return-to-operation conditions
- auditability
- traceability
- accountability
- evidence and logging requirements
- governance of increasingly autonomous AI systems

---

## Operational Human Oversight

A central research question is:

> How should meaningful human authority and intervention be embedded into the lifecycle of increasingly autonomous and agentic AI systems?

A simplified operational structure is:

**AI / Trust Event  
→ Human Intervention Trigger  
→ Escalation  
→ Qualified Human Review  
→ Human Authority / Override  
→ Evidence and Logging  
→ Resumption or Return-to-Operation**

This approach aims to translate general Human Oversight principles into operational governance mechanisms.
