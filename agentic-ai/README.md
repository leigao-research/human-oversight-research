# Agentic AI

This section documents research on Operational Human Oversight in agentic AI systems.

Current focus areas include:

- intervention triggers
- escalation criteria
- qualified human review
- human override
- temporary suspension
- resumption and rollback
- auditability and traceability
- accountability and evidence requirements
- bounded autonomy
- human-agent governance interfaces
