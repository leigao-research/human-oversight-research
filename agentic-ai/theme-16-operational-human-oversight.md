# Theme #16 — Operational Human Oversight Integration for Agentic AI Trust Systems

## Overview

This page documents the public research contribution proposed by **Lei Gao** as **Theme #16** within the public working process of the ITU-T Focus Group on Trustworthy and Intelligent Digital Architecture (FG-TIDA).

The theme focuses on a specific question:

> How can meaningful human intervention be operationally embedded into agentic AI trust architectures and trust lifecycles?

The objective is not to address AI governance in general, but to identify practical Human Oversight control points, intervention conditions, authority requirements, and evidence requirements for increasingly autonomous AI systems.

---

## Core Research Questions

The current work examines:

- What trust events should trigger human intervention?
- When should an AI system escalate to qualified human review?
- When should autonomous operation be temporarily held or suspended?
- What authority should a human reviewer possess?
- What information should be available to support meaningful intervention?
- When should human override be permitted?
- How should continuation, rollback, and resumption decisions be governed?
- What records should be preserved for auditability, traceability, and accountability?
- How can Human Oversight remain proportionate to risk and operationally scalable?

---

## Emerging Operational Structure

A developing Human Oversight structure links:

**Trust Event  
→ Intervention Trigger  
→ Escalation Level  
→ Human Authority  
→ Permitted Action  
→ Evidence / Logging Requirement  
→ Return-to-Operation Condition**

This structure is intended to help translate general principles of Human Oversight into operational governance requirements for agentic AI trust systems.

---

## Key Governance Mechanisms

Current mechanisms under examination include:

### Intervention Triggers
Conditions under which automated operation should no longer continue without human involvement.

### Escalation
Processes for moving from automated decision-making to qualified human review.

### Temporary Hold or Suspension
Mechanisms for pausing autonomous activity while uncertainty, conflict, abnormal behaviour, or trust degradation is reviewed.

### Human Override
Defined authority for human intervention where continued automated operation is inappropriate.

### Resumption and Rollback
Governance requirements for returning a system to operation or reversing previous automated actions.

### Evidence and Logging
Records required to support auditability, traceability, accountability, and post-event review.
